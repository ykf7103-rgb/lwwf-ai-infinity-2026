ASSESSMENT REFORM · NO-DICTATION POLICY

Why LWWF uses multimodal assessment instead of P1 traditional dictation

Not "no more vocabulary learning" · but no more rote dictation as a measure of students.

PILLAR ①

🔄 Free up learning space

Time goes into reading, writing, discussion and oral expression.

PILLAR ②

💪 Lower stress · Protect the joy of learning

Keep students loving the language.

PILLAR ③

🤖 Rethinking assessment in the AI era

Pure memorisation is less valuable · application, judgement and creation matter most.

PILLAR ④

📊 Multimodal assessment · Full picture of ability

Reading, writing, oral and project-based work assessed in parallel.
