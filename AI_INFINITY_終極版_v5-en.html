Global Research · WEF 2023 + OECD 2030

2027 Critical Skills

Five skills. Each one sits at the heart of AI-era education.

01 · ANALYTICAL

Analytical Thinking

Knowing what to ask. Knowing how to break a tangled problem apart. AI hands us answers — but only humans decide which questions are worth asking.

02 · CREATIVE

Creativity

AI scores eighty percent on autopilot. What separates the great from the merely good is human taste — a point of view AI cannot copy. Creativity becomes the rarest currency.

03 · AI LITERACY

AI & Big Data Literacy

Use AI. Question AI. Collaborate with AI. The skill isn't "chatting" — it's knowing when AI is wrong, and when its answers can be trusted.

04 · RESILIENCE

Resilience & Agility

AI never knows defeat. Students who can iterate through failure become the protagonists of their own stories. The ability to keep moving through uncertainty is the rarest trait of all.

05 · CURIOSITY

Curiosity + Self-Learning

Curiosity is the one human trait AI will never replace. It powers lifelong learning — the only real insurance against forty-four percent skill obsolescence.

📕 Modern Primary Mathematics 1A

❓ My child's English isn't that strong — will English Elite be too tough?

Class A admissions emphasise learning interest over "current level". The June interview assesses adaptability. If your child doesn't settle in, P.1 升 P.2 階段可以轉去 B/C 班，無壓力.

❓ B/C 班學生會唔會少咗英文資源？

No. 英文科本身全校統一教學時數同教材，B/C 班只係Math and Science taught in Chinese. All classes still have native-English reading lessons and daily English activities.

❓ Why can't students join English Elite after P.2 → P.3?

Because from P.3 onwards, the accumulated knowledge in English-medium subjects已經唔少（數學 word problems、科學實驗報告）.中途插入會令學生壓力大.Mid-year entrants are handled case-by-case.

❓ Math用英文教，會唔會影響中文數學概念？

No. 中文版同英文版工作紙內容完全一致（見左圖），老師教課時雙語切換.學生兩種Languages嘅數學概念都熟練.

ASSESSMENT REFORM

Why LWWF　Dropped Traditional Dictation

From assessing memorisation to assessing application — answering what the AI era actually demands.梁校自 2024-25 學年逐步推行無考默政策，中文、英文科不再用傳統默書作為定期評估.

📌

並非「不再學生字」

而係「不用機械式默寫去評估學生」

學生仍然要學中文字、識生字、會運用——但唔再用機械式默寫測驗去量度成效.

2024
推行學年
中 + 英
取消科目
Pillar 1

🔄 釋出學習空間　·　深層發展

將原本俾默書準備同測驗嘅時間，用於reading comprehension, writing, and discussion、口語表達 — building deeper language ability.香港 2025 年教育局已認可呢個方向.

Pillar 2

💪 Lower Pressure · Protect the Joy of Learning

Traditional dictation breeds resistance to Chinese and English——每週多次默書，分數低就被罰. Without it, students 持續喜歡語文 — the foundation of lifelong learning.

Pillar 3

🤖 The AI Era · Rethinking Assessment

當 AI 可以即時提供答案、自動拼字、語音輸入，純粹「記得字點寫」嘅實際價值大幅下降 — what truly matters is application, judgement, and creation同分辨 AI 對錯嘅能力.

NO-DICTATION · STUDENT GAINS + PARENT ROLE

Students ×Parents— A Win-Win

Dropping dictation doesn't mean parents step back — it means the role shifts. From supervising memorisation to reading and learning together.

STUDENT VIEW

😊 What They Gain

  • Lower Learning Stress — no more dictation anxiety
  • Stronger Reading Interest — from "forced to memorise" to "choosing to read"
  • Better Expression — built through discussion and writing
  • More Flexible Time — room to pursue interests and strengths

"I no longer dread dictation days — I actually look forward to Chinese class." — Student

PARENT VIEW

🤝 Shifting Roles

  • From "supervising" to "reading together" — parent-child reading replaces dictation drilling
  • Use the AI Magic Dictation tool (introduced earlier) as a self-study companion
  • Focus on interest rather than test scores
  • Attend home-school workshops — learn AI alongside your child

The deeper parents engage, the more students gain.

Core Principle: "no dictation" doesn't mean "no assessment" — see the next page on our three-track assessment framework.
