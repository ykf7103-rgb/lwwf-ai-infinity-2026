2027 Critical Skills

Five skills. Each one sits at the heart of AI-era education.

01 · ANALYTICAL

Analytical Thinking

Knowing what to ask. Knowing how to break a tangled problem apart. AI hands us answers — but only humans decide which questions are worth asking.

02 · CREATIVE

Creativity

AI scores eighty percent on autopilot. What separates the great from the merely good is human taste — a point of view AI cannot copy. Creativity becomes the rarest currency.

03 · AI LITERACY

AI & Big Data Literacy

Use AI. Question AI. Collaborate with AI. The skill isn't "chatting" — it's knowing when AI is wrong, and when its answers can be trusted.

04 · RESILIENCE

Resilience & Agility

AI never knows defeat. Students who can iterate through failure become the protagonists of their own stories. The ability to keep moving through uncertainty is the rarest trait of all.

05 · CURIOSITY

Curiosity + Self-Learning

Curiosity is the one human trait AI will never replace. It powers lifelong learning — the only real insurance against forty-four percent skill obsolescence.

❓ My child's English isn't strong enough — will English Elite be too hard?

Class A admissions emphasise learning interestrather than "current level". The June interview assesses adaptability. If students don't settle in, students can transfer to Class B/C between P.1 and P.2，無壓力.

❓ Will Class B/C students get less English exposure?

No. the English subject itself is identical school-wide (same hours, same materials)，B/C 班只係Math and Scienceare taught in Chinese. All classes still have NET reading lessons and daily English activities.

❓ Why can't students join English Elite after P.2 → P.3?

Because from P.3 onwards, the accumulated knowledge in English-medium subjectsis substantial (math word problems, lab reports). Joining mid-stream creates too much pressure.Mid-year entrants are handled case-by-case.

❓ Will teaching Math in English affect Chinese math concepts?

No. Chinese and English versions of the worksheets內容完全一致 (see the photo on the left); teachers switch between languages while teaching. Students master math concepts in both languages.

ASSESSMENT REFORM

Why LWWF　Dropped Traditional Dictation

Moving from "memorisation testing" to "application testing" — answering what the AI era actually demands. LWWF has, since 2024-25 academic yearhas gradually rolled out the no-dictation policy; 中文、英文科no longer uses traditional dictation as a regular assessment.

📌

Not "no more learning characters"

而係「不用機械式默寫去評估學生」

Students still learn characters, know vocabulary, and use it — we just stop using rote dictation tests to measure progress.

2024
Launch Year
Chinese + English
Subjects Removed
Pillar 1

🔄 Free Up Learning Space · Go Deeper

We redirect the time previously spent on dictation prep and tests into reading comprehension, writing, and discussion、speaking — building deeper language ability. The Hong Kong EDB endorsed this direction in 2025.

Pillar 2

💪 Lower Pressure · Protect Learning Interest

Traditional dictation breeds resistance to Chinese and English — multiple weekly dictations, with low scores punished. Without it, students keep loving the language — the foundation of lifelong learning.

Pillar 3

🤖 The AI Era · Rethinking Assessment

When AI can answer instantly, autocomplete spelling, and transcribe speech, the practical value of "remembering how to write a character" collapses — what truly matters is application, judgement, and creation同分辨 AI 對錯嘅能力.

Pillar 4

📊 Multi-Track Assessment · Reflects Real Ability

We use reading comprehension、writing、speaking、class performance、project work — diverse methods that align with real language use. Paired with the Three-Track Assessment.

Three Tracks,　Full Picture

Not abandoning assessment — moving from paper-and-pen alone to three parallel tracks. This closes the AI-ghostwriting blind spot and truly reflects ability.

🔬 Performance Assessment in Action

Performance + Oral Defence — students build and explain it themselves; AI ghostwriting is impossible

📝
Track 1 · Knowledge Base

Paper & Pen

Traditional tests and exams stay — covering core knowledge, computation, and writing.

Best for: core concepts · foundational skills · 30-40% weight

📂
Track 2 · Learning Process

Process Portfolio

Collects a term of work, reflections, and progress, capturing Learning Process, not just the result.Includes drafts, revisions, and self- and peer-assessment.

Best for: creative work · writing · PBL · 30-35% weight
